## Proposed terms (subject to legal review)

1. **Contributions.** "Contribution" means the original code, documents, designs or other material the Contributor intentionally submits for inclusion in Cryptex, identified by their submitted GitHub pull requests, commits, or agreed written records. The agreement should specify its effective date and whether it covers earlier submissions.
2. **Retention of ownership.** The Contributor keeps copyright ownership of their contributions. This draft does **not** transfer title or assign all copyrights to the maintainer.
3. **Copyright license.** The Contributor proposes to grant the named recipient a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable (to the extent legally permissible), transferable and sublicensable copyright license to reproduce, prepare derivative works, publicly display, distribute, commercialize and relicense the Contributions, alone or combined with other works, under open-source or proprietary terms. The final legal text must address moral rights and any additional formalities required by applicable law.
4. **Patent matters.** Counsel should determine whether an express patent license, its scope, termination and defensive provisions are appropriate. No patent grant should be inferred from this draft.
5. **Authority and originality.** The Contributor proposes to represent that they have the right to submit the Contributions; they will disclose employer, client, co-author and third-party rights or obligations that could affect those permissions; and they will identify any third-party material and applicable license notices.
6. **No obligation to merge or pay.** The maintainer need not accept or use a Contribution; absent a separate written agreement, submissions do not create employment or payment rights.
7. **Existing licenses remain in effect.** No new CLA changes or revokes licenses already validly granted to third parties under existing Cryptex open-source releases. The maintainer must still respect third-party and other contributor rights.
8. **No trademark permission.** Copyright permissions do not confer ownership or licensed use of the Cryptex wordmark, logo, or any third-party trademark.
9. **Governing law / dispute resolution.** Proposed Ontario, Canada — **requires counsel to confirm** suitability, enforceability, and international-contributor provisions.
10. **Consent and evidence.** Counsel-approved signing must record the exact agreement version, identities, date, assent, and covered contributions; a PR checkbox is not a substitute for an implemented, reviewed consent and recordkeeping process.
