## Interpretation boundary

At this 100k macOS point, the segment candidate again showed lower append time, enumeration time, rebuild time and allocated KiB than the one-file-per-event candidate. The repository crash probes also behaved as designed for their synthetic interruption model.

No architecture decision is made from this run. P0-1 still requires:

- 500k evidence on macOS and Windows
- initial and incremental Syncthing measurements
- Windows physical allocation evidence
- purge-complexity analysis
- corruption-blast-radius analysis
- ADR-0013 review and acceptance
