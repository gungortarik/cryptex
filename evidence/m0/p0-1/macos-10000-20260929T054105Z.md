## Interpretation boundary

At this single 10k pilot point, the segment candidate showed lower append time, enumeration time, rebuild time and allocated KiB than the one-file-per-event candidate. This is a preliminary signal only.

No architecture decision is made from this run. P0-1 still requires:

- matching Windows 10k evidence
- 100k and 500k evidence on macOS and Windows
- initial and incremental Syncthing measurements
- Windows physical allocation evidence
- full crash-behavior evidence
- purge-complexity analysis
- corruption-blast-radius analysis
- ADR-0013 review and acceptance
