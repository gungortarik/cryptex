## Interpretation boundary

At this 500k Windows point, the segment candidate again showed lower append time, enumeration time and rebuild time than the one-file-per-event candidate. Verified file-allocation measurements also show materially different filesystem allocation overhead between the two retained corpora. This remains evidence, not an architecture decision.

No architecture decision is made from this run. P0-1 still requires:

- Windows directory-metadata overhead evidence or an explicitly documented measurement limitation
- initial and incremental Syncthing measurements
- purge-complexity analysis
- corruption-blast-radius analysis
- ADR-0013 review and acceptance
