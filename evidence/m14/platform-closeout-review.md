## Security and recovery review

- The [first Mac startup regression](https://github.com/gungortarik/cryptex/actions/runs/45044243171/job/137275224639) returned `existing_profile_settings_restricted_peer`. After a confirmed device restriction, the settings read returned `Attention / Start`, one device and the saved revision. The test requires `Paused` and an empty device list. This is evidence of an incorrect settings response. The test did not observe an unauthorized transfer. The same symptom is recorded in the earlier [window restoration handoff](../../docs/34_CURRENT_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF.md#m14-window-restoration). Investigate the settings controller's generation check and transport identity observation across the restriction before treating this intermittent result as resolved.
- A non-pilot Desktop still accepts a developer-selected executable through its filesystem start control. The installed supervisor starts the file watcher on the ordinary path. The renderer-side command's sidecar check does not protect that path. The existing ADR-0012 production gate requires constraining or gating this control before shipping product supervision. See [the watcher](../../apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/main.rs) and the [accepted decision](../../docs/adr/ADR-0012-background-runtime-implementation-proposal.md#mandatory-production-gates-retained).
- The [local updater](../../apps/updater/src/main.rs) reports a 30-second probe timeout and asks for Desktop to quit. It does not terminate or reap the timed-out probe on that branch. A probe that remains alive with its session lock can delay recovery. The interruption rehearsal releases its held probe before retrying. It establishes recovery after that exit. Automatic recovery from an indefinitely hung probe is unverified.
- A process kill after package selection exercises the on-disk journal and the next invocation. It does not model a filesystem losing unflushed changes in a power failure. Signed distribution and notarization belong to M17 under the [M14 contract](../../docs/milestones/M14_PLATFORM_POLISH_UPDATES.md).
