The security review traced owner commands through authenticated Desktop IPC and their operation-specific capabilities. The daemon signs the inventory. The packaged encryptor takes a public recipient. The offline tool holds the separate first-device signing credential and writes an advanced Kit only after confirmation. The recovered reader authenticates the archive against the approved checkpoint. Its first read holds the new device's canonical locks and checks its empty stream and projection before showing archived notes. Personal-profile backup and recovery writes remain disabled.
